About—

Tom Eisenbraun dropped out of the gifted & talented program in search of first principles, instead became the man on the dump, whispering: 'invisible priest!' beneath the blatter of grackles, ensued long evenings imagining the breeze through taut strings, seeking out that one thing, that it would be enough.

If you seek a better answer, you'll need to learn to ask a better question. Better still yet, where was it one first heard of the truth?
